Privacy policy

How KCGLOBAL Worldwide handles information about you when you use this website. It is short because this site collects very little.

Last updated 19 August 2026

The short version

  • We set no cookies, and run no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts of any kind.
  • The only personal information you can give us here is what you choose to type into the enquiry form, or send us by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade anyone's personal information.
  • Simply loading a page shares your IP address with our host and with the services that deliver our fonts, photographs and map — as it would on almost any website. Section 6 names every one of them.

1 Who we are

KCGLOBAL Worldwide is a real-estate firm based in Lagos State, Nigeria. When this policy says we, us or our, it means KCGLOBAL Worldwide. For the purposes of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, we are the data controller for the information described here.

2 What this policy covers

This policy covers this website and the enquiries that reach us through it. It does not attempt to describe everything we do with information once you become a client — a tenancy, a sale or a management instruction each carry their own paperwork, and the terms of that engagement govern it.

Our site links out to WhatsApp and shows a Google map. Once you leave this site or interact with an embedded service, that company's own privacy policy applies, not ours.

3 Information you give us

The enquiry form on our contact page asks for four things, all of which you type yourself:

  • Your full name — so we know who we are replying to.
  • Your email address — so we can reply.
  • Your phone number — because most property questions are settled faster on a call than in writing.
  • Your message — whatever you choose to tell us.

If you open a listing and choose Request a callback, the form arrives with the name of that listing already written into the message box. That is done in your browser, from the link you clicked. Nothing is recorded about which listings you looked at.

The form sends your enquiry through WhatsApp. When you press Send via WhatsApp, nothing is transmitted from this website. Your browser opens WhatsApp with the four answers above written into a message, addressed to our business number. You can read it, change it, or close it without sending. Nothing reaches us until you press send in WhatsApp, and at that point WhatsApp is carrying the message, so Meta's privacy policy applies to it as well as ours.

You may also reach us by phone, by WhatsApp or by email directly. In those cases we receive whatever that service passes to us — typically your number or email address, and the content of your message.

Please do not send us documents or identification through the form. If we need title documents, identification or anything similar, we will tell you and agree a sensible way to send it.

4 Information collected automatically

We do not run analytics on this site. We cannot tell you how many people visited it, which pages they read, or where they came from, because we do not measure any of it.

Our hosting provider does keep standard server logs, as every web host does. These typically record the IP address a request came from, the page requested, the time, and the browser and operating system reported by that browser. Those logs exist so the service can be operated and defended against abuse. We do not use them to build any picture of you.

5 Cookies

This site sets no cookies. It stores nothing on your device — no cookies, no local storage, no session storage, no advertising or analytics identifiers. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

The third-party services in the next section may set their own cookies when your browser contacts them. We have no control over those and receive nothing from them.

6 Third-party services

Loading a web page means your browser fetches files from wherever they are stored, and the company storing them necessarily sees your IP address in order to send them. These are every such service this site uses:

Vercel
Hosts the site and serves every page. Receives your IP address and request details in its server logs.
Google Fonts
Delivers the two typefaces the site is set in. Your browser requests them from Google on every page, which discloses your IP address to Google.
Unsplash
Hosts the large banner photographs at the top of our pages. Requesting them discloses your IP address to Unsplash.
Google Maps
Provides the map on the contact page only. It is an embedded frame from Google and may set Google's own cookies when it loads.
WhatsApp (only if you choose to send)
Our WhatsApp buttons are ordinary links, and the enquiry form opens WhatsApp with a message written for you. Nothing reaches WhatsApp or Meta until you press send there.

There are no advertising networks, no social media pixels, no session recording and no chat widgets on this site.

7 How we use your information

We use what you send us in order to:

  • reply to your enquiry and answer your question;
  • arrange an inspection, a call or a consultation you have asked for;
  • send you details of a property you have asked about;
  • keep a record of what was discussed and agreed, so that we can act on it consistently.

We do not add you to a marketing list because you made an enquiry. If we ever want to send you anything beyond a reply to what you asked, we will ask you first, and you can say no without it affecting the service you receive.

8 Our lawful basis

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, personal data must be processed on a lawful basis. Ours are:

  • Your request. When you send an enquiry, you are asking us to act on it, and we process what you sent in order to do so — as steps taken at your request before entering a contract.
  • Contract. Where we go on to act for you, we process what is necessary to perform that agreement.
  • Legitimate interests. Keeping our site running, secure and free of abuse, and keeping a record of our own dealings.
  • Legal obligation. Where a law or a regulator requires us to keep or produce a record.

9 Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share it only where one of these applies:

  • Service providers who operate parts of our business on our instruction — for example the company that hosts this website or handles our email. They may only use it to provide that service to us.
  • Professional advisers — such as a surveyor, a solicitor or a registry search agent — where a transaction you have asked us to progress requires it, and only so far as it requires it.
  • Where the law requires it, or to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.

10 How long we keep it

We keep what you send us for as long as it takes to deal with your enquiry, and then for as long as we may reasonably need it — to pick up a conversation you come back to, to evidence what was agreed, or because a law requires it.

CONFIRM: the client should set an actual period here — for example, enquiries that do not become instructions deleted after 24 months, and transaction records kept for the period Nigerian law and their insurers require. We have deliberately not invented a figure.

You can ask us to delete what we hold about you at any time. See section 13.

11 Where it is processed

This website is hosted outside Nigeria, and the font, image and map services in section 6 are operated by companies based outside Nigeria. Using the site therefore involves your information being processed abroad.

The Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 permits such transfers where an adequate level of protection is in place. Where we choose a provider, we look for one that offers contractual data-protection terms.

12 Keeping it safe

This site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), so what you type into the form is encrypted in transit. We restrict access to enquiries to the people who need to act on them.

No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we would rather say so than promise otherwise. Please do not send us bank details, passwords or identity documents by email or through the form.

13 Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you have the right to:

  • Be told what personal data we hold about you and why.
  • Get a copy of it.
  • Have it corrected if it is wrong or incomplete.
  • Have it deleted, where we have no continuing reason to keep it.
  • Restrict or object to how we use it.
  • Receive it in a portable form, or have it sent to someone else, where that applies.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we relied on consent.
  • Complain — see section 16.

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details in section 1. We will not charge you for it, and we will respond within the time the Act allows. We may need to confirm who you are first, so that we do not hand your information to someone else.

14 Children

This site is intended for adults dealing in property. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, tell us and we will delete it.

15 Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and change the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will say what changed rather than leave you to spot it.

16 Questions and complaints

Ask us first — most things are settled in one conversation. Call 08030555002, email info@kcglobalworld.com, or write to us at the address in section 1.

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled it, you may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC), the authority responsible for data protection in Nigeria.
