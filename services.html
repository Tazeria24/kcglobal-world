What we do

Five services, one standard

Property Management · Building Contractor · Property Development · Lease & Let · Sales of Landed Properties. Each is a complete service — and they are built to work together.

How we engage

One firm, from the search to the statement

Most Lagos property problems come from the handovers — between the agent, the lawyer, the builder and the manager. We cover the whole chain, so nothing is anyone else's problem.

01

Property Management

Full-service management for landlords and investors: rent collection, tenant relations, maintenance, and reporting — handled transparently on your behalf.

Management is where trust is either earned or quietly lost. Our answer is written reporting: every statement shows what came in, what went out, and why. You can be entirely hands-off without ever being in the dark — which is the point, especially if you are not in Lagos.

What's included

  • Tenant sourcing, screening and referencing
  • Lease preparation, renewals and rent reviews
  • Rent collection and prompt remittance
  • Routine and emergency maintenance
  • Service-charge administration and reconciliation
  • Periodic inspections with written reports
  • Arrears management and recovery
  • Statutory payments and compliance follow-up

Best for: landlords with one property or a portfolio, and owners living outside Nigeria who need someone accountable on the ground.

02

Property Development

Turning land into value. We plan, finance, and deliver residential and mixed-use developments to a high standard.

A development is won or lost on the appraisal, long before the first block is laid. We cost the scheme honestly against real Lagos build rates and real sale values, and if the numbers do not work we will tell you that rather than build something that cannot be sold at the price it needs.

What's included

  • Site identification and feasibility appraisal
  • Title verification and due diligence
  • Concept design and planning approvals
  • Development budget and cash-flow planning
  • Contractor procurement and supervision
  • Sales or letting strategy on completion
  • Handover, snagging and documentation
  • Joint-venture structuring where landowners partner with us

Best for: landowners sitting on undeveloped plots, and investors looking for a scheme run end to end rather than piecemeal.

03

Building Contractor

Quality construction from foundation to finish, with experienced project supervision and honest timelines.

We price against a bill of quantities before anyone breaks ground, and we hand over a written programme with dates we intend to keep. Progress is reported with dated photographs throughout — you should never have to wonder what your money has bought between one payment and the next.

What's included

  • Drawings, approvals and permits
  • Priced bill of quantities before work starts
  • Material procurement and quality control
  • Site supervision and programme management
  • Progress reporting with dated photographs
  • Renovation and remodelling of existing structures
  • Snagging and formal handover
  • Defects liability period after completion

Best for: anyone building on land they already own, and owners refurbishing a property before letting or sale.

04

Lease & Let

Matching quality tenants to quality properties, and managing leases so both sides are protected.

A property let quickly to the wrong tenant costs more than one that sat empty for a month. We screen properly, document properly, and price to the market rather than to a hopeful number — then handle the renewal before it becomes a void.

What's included

  • Rental valuation and market pricing
  • Marketing and qualified viewings
  • Tenant screening and referencing
  • Negotiation of terms
  • Tenancy agreements and inventories
  • Deposit handling and check-in
  • Renewals, rent reviews and re-letting
  • Corporate and expatriate lettings

Best for: landlords letting residential or commercial space, and companies sourcing accommodation for staff.

05

Sales of Landed Properties

Verified plots and homes across Lagos, with clean titles and guidance through every step of the purchase.

Every plot we list is checked before it reaches you: the title document confirmed, the survey verified at the state registry, and a search run to make sure the land is free of government acquisition or dispute. We tell you exactly which title a property carries before you pay anything.

What's included

  • Registry search and title verification
  • Survey plan confirmation
  • Physical inspection and neighbourhood assessment
  • Price guidance against recent comparables
  • Negotiation on your behalf
  • Deed of assignment and consent processing
  • Payment structuring and receipting
  • Handover and post-purchase documentation

Best for: first-time buyers who want the checking done for them, and diaspora buyers who need someone to inspect and verify in person.

Fees

Priced in writing, before anything begins

Management is charged as an agreed percentage of rent collected, so our income only rises when yours does.

Sales and lettings carry standard agency and legal fees, quoted up front against the transaction value.

Construction and development are quoted per project against a priced bill of quantities.

Every figure is put in writing before work begins, and we do not add charges mid-way through an instruction.
