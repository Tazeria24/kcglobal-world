After you send it

What happens next

  1. We read it properly

    Your enquiry goes to someone who can actually answer it, not a queue. If it is about a property, we confirm availability before replying.

  2. We come back with specifics

    Availability, the title a property carries, what the fees will be, and what we would need from you — not a brochure.

  3. We arrange the inspection

    By appointment, including weekends, with someone senior present. Abroad? We inspect for you and send dated photographs and video.

  4. You decide, unhurried

    Everything in writing before anything is paid. If it is not right, we would rather you walked away and came back for the next one.
