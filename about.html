About the firm

The firm behind the properties

KCGLOBAL Worldwide is a Lagos real-estate firm built for clients who want their property decisions handled properly — and want to be told the truth about them.

Who we are

An end-to-end real-estate service, handled properly

KCGLOBAL Worldwide is a Lagos-based real-estate firm offering an end-to-end service — from managing and leasing properties to developing land and delivering quality construction. We work with buyers, landlords, and investors who expect their property decisions handled with care, clarity, and professionalism.

Most of what goes wrong in Nigerian property goes wrong early — a title that was never properly searched, a budget agreed verbally, a tenant placed in a hurry, a building priced before anyone costed it honestly. We built this firm around the opposite habit: do the unglamorous checking first, put the numbers in writing, and only then move.

That is why we operate across the whole chain rather than one slice of it. The team that verifies a title is the team that can develop the land, build on it, let it, and manage it afterwards. You deal with one firm, and one account of what is happening — not four parties each blaming the others.

Our office sits on Oteyi Garden Estate Road in Abule-Ado, and most of our work runs across the Lekki–Ajah corridor, Ikoyi, Victoria Island, and the Abule-Ado and Festac axis. A good share of our clients are outside Nigeria and rely on us to be their eyes on the ground.

How we work

Four steps, on every instruction

The same sequence whether you are buying one plot or handing us a portfolio to manage.

  1. Brief

    We start with what you actually want — budget, timeline, and how hands-on you intend to be. If what you are asking for is not realistic at that price in that location, we say so at this stage rather than three months in.

  2. Verify

    Titles searched at the registry, surveys checked, structures inspected, costs priced against a bill of quantities. Nothing is recommended to you before it has been checked by us.

  3. Execute

    Documentation, negotiation, construction or letting — carried out to the written programme and the written price agreed at the outset.

  4. Report

    Written updates showing what came in, what was spent, and why. You should be able to step back from a property without ever being in the dark about it.
