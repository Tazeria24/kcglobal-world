Why choose us

Six reasons clients stay with us

Anyone can promise transparency. What follows is what we actually do differently, and what you are entitled to hold us to.

The difference

Not claims — practices

Each of these is something we do on every instruction, not a value on a wall. The line in italics is the test you can hold us to.

  • Years of Experience

    A track record across management, sales, and development in the Lagos market. That breadth matters more than it sounds: the person advising you on a plot has also built on one, let one, and managed the aftermath — so the advice accounts for what happens after the purchase, not just up to it.

    Ask us for comparable work we have done in your area before you commit.

  • Trusted Professionals

    A team that treats your property and capital as their own. In practice that means we will talk you out of a purchase we do not believe in, and we will tell you when the cheaper option is the right one — including when it costs us the larger fee.

    We would rather lose an instruction than place you in a bad one.

  • Transparent Process

    Clear terms, honest pricing, and no surprises. Fees, build costs and agency charges are put in writing before work begins, and the title a property carries is stated plainly before any money moves.

    No charge is added mid-way through an instruction. Ever.

  • Customer Satisfaction

    Relationships that outlast the transaction. Most of our work comes from people we have already acted for, or from someone they sent — which only happens if the second year is as well handled as the first week.

    You keep the same point of contact after the deal closes.

  • Quality Construction

    Built to standard, delivered on schedule. Work is priced against a bill of quantities before ground is broken, supervised on site rather than by phone, and handed over with a snagging list closed out.

    Dated progress photographs, sent throughout the build.

  • Reliable Property Management

    Consistent, accountable management you can step back from. Rent collected and remitted, maintenance handled, tenants managed — with a written statement showing what came in, what was spent, and why.

    Written reporting, wherever in the world you are.

What we will not do

A firm is defined as much by what it refuses as by what it offers. These are the things we do not do, whatever the fee attached.

  • List land we have not searched at the registry ourselves.
  • Quote a build price before it has been costed against a bill of quantities.
  • Take a management instruction we do not have the capacity to service properly.
  • Place a tenant we have not screened because the unit is sitting empty.
  • Work outside the areas of Lagos we know well enough to advise on honestly.
  • Tell you a timeline we already know we cannot keep.

Verified before it reaches you

Every plot we list has had its title document confirmed, its survey verified at the state registry, and a search run against government acquisition or dispute. We deal in Certificates of Occupancy, Governor's Consent, and registered Deeds of Assignment — and we name which one applies before you pay anything.

If we get it wrong

Tell us directly, on either number. Problems handled late are what turn a small repair into a lost tenant, and a query into a dispute. We would rather hear it in week one.
