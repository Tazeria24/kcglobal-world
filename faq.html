Frequently asked

Grouped by what you are trying to do

Fees, titles, timelines and how we work with clients abroad. Every answer here is one we are happy to be held to.

Buying land and titles

Are your landed properties verified, and what titles do you sell?

Every plot we list is checked before it reaches you — we confirm the title document, verify the survey at the state registry, and run a search to make sure the land is free of government acquisition or dispute. We deal in Certificates of Occupancy (C-of-O), Governor's Consent, and registered Deeds of Assignment, and we tell you exactly which one a property carries before you pay anything.

Can I inspect a property before committing?

Always, and we encourage it. Inspections are by appointment so the property is presented properly and someone senior can answer your questions on site. Call either number or send a WhatsApp message and we will arrange a time, including weekends.

Managing and building

What does your property management service actually cover?

Rent collection and remittance, tenant sourcing and screening, lease preparation and renewals, routine and emergency maintenance, service-charge administration, and periodic inspections. You receive a written statement showing what came in, what was spent, and why — so you can be hands-off without being in the dark.

Do you build on land I already own?

Yes. We take projects from foundation to finish, including drawings and approvals, costing, procurement, and site supervision, and we hand over a written programme with realistic dates. You get progress reporting throughout rather than silence between payments.

Fees

How much do you charge, and when?

Management is charged as an agreed percentage of rent collected, so our income only rises when yours does. Sales and lettings carry standard agency and legal fees, and construction is quoted per project against a priced bill of quantities. Every figure is put in writing before work begins — we do not add charges mid-way.

Working with us

I live abroad. Can you handle a purchase or manage my property for me?

Yes — a good share of our clients are outside Nigeria. We carry out inspections on your behalf and send dated photos and video, handle verification and documentation, and can act under a Power of Attorney where you prefer. For managed properties you get the same written reporting wherever you are, and we work to your timezone for calls.

Which parts of Lagos do you cover?

We work across Lagos, with most activity on the Lekki–Ajah corridor, Ikoyi, Victoria Island, and the Abule-Ado and Festac axis where our office sits. If a property falls outside our usual patch we will say so rather than take on something we cannot service properly.

How quickly will I hear back?

Same working day for calls and WhatsApp messages in nearly every case, and within one working day for the contact form. If a question needs a document check or a registry search before we can answer honestly, we will tell you that and come back with the answer rather than guess.
