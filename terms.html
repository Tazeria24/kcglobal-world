2 Listings are information, not offers

Every property shown on this site is an invitation to enquire. It is not an offer capable of acceptance, and browsing, saving or messaging us about a listing creates no contract between us.

3 Accuracy of what you see

We take care with what we publish, but descriptions, measurements, dimensions, specifications, plot sizes and photographs are provided as a general guide. They are not a survey, a valuation, or a statement of fact you should rely on alone.

Photographs may be taken with a wide lens, may date from before a property was occupied, and may not reflect its present condition. Where a photograph is indicative rather than of the specific unit, we say so on the page.

Verify before you commit. Inspect the property, commission your own survey where appropriate, and have your own solicitor confirm the title. We will help you do all three — see the buying process on our properties page.

4 Title and documents

Where a listing names a title type — a Certificate of Occupancy, a Governor's Consent, a deed of assignment — that is our understanding of the position based on the documents we have seen. It is not a warranty of good title.

Title in Lagos is a matter of record, and records must be checked. Any purchase should be preceded by a search at the relevant registry, carried out by your own solicitor. We will make the documents available for that search.

Do not use the form or our numbers to send unlawful, abusive or misleading content, or unsolicited marketing.
